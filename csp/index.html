THE ISSUE AROUND US

What is air pollution?

Air pollution happens when harmful substances such as smoke, dust, and gases enter the air and affect people and the environment.

⌁

Common causes

Vehicle exhaust, road and construction dust, burning waste, industrial emissions, and the use of certain fuels can reduce local air quality.

♡

Possible effects

Polluted air can irritate the eyes and throat, worsen breathing problems, affect heart and lung health, and harm plants and ecosystems.

✳

How we can help

Use public transport when possible, maintain vehicles, avoid burning waste, save energy, plant and protect trees, and report persistent pollution sources.
